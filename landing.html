FAQ

Common questions.

Is LedgerLoop approved by HMRC?
No software is "HMRC-approved" for Self Assessment — including major accounting suites. What LedgerLoop produces are accurate estimates mapped to the boxes on your HMRC tax return. Every rate we use is verified from gov.uk. We always recommend you double-check with HMRC or a qualified accountant before submitting your return.
How do I get my CSV from eBay / Etsy / Depop?
eBay: Seller Hub → Payments → Reports → Transaction report. Pick a date range.
Etsy: Shop Manager → Settings → Options → Download Data → Orders CSV.
Depop: Your seller dashboard → sales download (3-month chunks, upload multiple for a full year).
Drop the resulting file on the LedgerLoop import page. We auto-detect the platform.
What if I sell on multiple platforms?
Upload as many CSVs as you have. We dedupe by order number, so re-uploading the same file is safe. Your dashboard and tax page combine everything across all three platforms automatically.
Can I get a refund?
Yes — 7-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans, no questions asked. Data is encrypted and hosted in the UK; payments go through Stripe.
