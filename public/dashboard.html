Breakdown

COGS Cost of Goods Sold — the price you paid for items, summed across everything that sold during the year. The single biggest deductible expense for most resellers. Only items where you've entered a cost on the Inventory tab count here. £6,200.00
Direct sale costs Direct sale costs are anything that varies per sale: marketplace selling fees, payment processing fees, postage labels you paid for, and refunds you issued. These come straight off your gross sale price and are pulled automatically from your CSV imports. £2,800.00
General expenses General expenses aren't tied to one specific item — packaging supplies, mileage to source stock, your accounting subscription, the use-of-home flat-rate, phone bills for the business etc. Log them on the Expenses page (coming soon) and they'll flow into Box 20 here. £900.00
Income tax (marginal) Marginal income tax means the tax is calculated at the rate of whichever band your reselling profit falls into once stacked on top of your PAYE salary. If your day job already fills the basic-rate band (£12,570–£50,270), your reselling profit gets taxed at the 40% higher rate. If you have no PAYE, the first £12,570 of profit is tax-free.Source: gov.uk/income-tax-rates £1,720.00
Class 4 NIC Class 4 National Insurance is a percentage of your self-employed profit that goes to HMRC alongside income tax. 6% on profit between £12,570 and £50,270, then 2% on profit above that. PAYE-only earners pay Class 1 via their payslip — Class 4 only applies to your self-employed slice.Source: gov.uk/self-employed-national-insurance-rates £0.00
Student loan Student loan repayment on the trading profit portion, on top of whatever your PAYE already deducts. Each plan has a different income threshold and rate (mostly 9%; Postgrad is 6%). Set yours under Tax inputs above — or change to "No student loan" if you've paid yours off.Source: gov.uk/repaying-your-student-loan £774.00
